# HCI Co-Design Canvas

## Patient App, Registration Kiosk, Nurse Dashboard

## Canvas #01: Problem Space

### What problem are you solving?

---

# 1. USER

## Primary Users

### Patients

**Characteristics**

* ED patients arriving through walk-in, ambulance, or wearable-connected pathways.
* May experience pain, anxiety, uncertainty, or long waiting times.
* Have different levels of digital literacy.
* May or may not own smartphones.

**Needs**

* Clear communication during the ED process.
* Understanding of their current status.
* Reassurance while waiting.
* Access to information regardless of device ownership.
* Simple explanations of health information.

**Goals**

### Short-Term Goals

* Register quickly.
* Understand what happens next.
* Receive updates while waiting.
* Understand basic information from their wearable data.

### Long-Term Goals

* Feel more confident and informed during ED visits.
* Improve trust in the healthcare process.
* Reduce anxiety caused by uncertainty.

---

## Secondary Users

### Nurses and Doctors

**Characteristics**

* Work under time pressure.
* Need rapid access to patient information.
* Make final clinical decisions.
* Require reliable, low-friction tools.

**Needs**

* Clear patient summaries.
* Updated vital trends.
* ESI recommendations with confidence indicators.
* Ability to override automated suggestions.

**Goals**

### Short-Term Goals

* Identify deterioration earlier.
* Improve patient prioritisation.
* Reduce repeated manual information gathering.

### Long-Term Goals

* Improve ED workflow.
* Support continuous re-triage.
* Improve patient safety.

---

# 2. SYSTEM

## Tasks

### Short-Term

The HCI system performs:

* Digital registration.
* Patient onboarding.
* Wristband pairing.
* Display of patient updates.
* Presentation of vital trends.
* Delivery of educational information.
* Nurse dashboard support.

### Long-Term

The system supports:

* Continuous re-triage.
* Improved patient engagement.
* Digital transformation of Mercer’s paper workflow.
* Better communication between patients and clinicians.

---

# Advantages of HCI System

Compared with a fully manual process:

* Reduces uncertainty during waiting.
* Makes patient information easier to access.
* Supports patients without smartphones through kiosks.
* Provides consistent explanations.
* Reduces unnecessary staff interruptions.

---

# Interaction Capabilities

## User Emotional Response

The system should create:

* Trust.
* Calmness.
* Understanding.
* Confidence.

It should avoid:

* Medical overload.
* False reassurance.
* Replacing clinician communication.
